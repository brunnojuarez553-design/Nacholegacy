PRIVACY AT A GLANCE

We collect only the information needed to answer inquiries, assess vehicle damage, coordinate repairs and understand how our website is used.

Privacy questions ↗

Effective and last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Nacho’s Legacy Body Shop (“Nacho’s Legacy,” “we,” “us” or “our”) handles personal information submitted through nachoslegacy.com, our vehicle assessment form, virtual assistant and direct communications. Our shop is located at 27 Third Street, Lansdowne, PA 19050.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect your name, telephone number, email address, preferred language, vehicle year, make and model, details about damage or an accident, insurance or claim information, preferred contact method, messages and photographs you choose to upload.

We also receive limited technical and usage information, such as device and browser type, pages viewed, referring source, approximate location derived from an IP address, interactions with contact buttons and security logs. We do not request payment card details through the website.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to questions and schedule estimates or appointments.
  • Review a vehicle for an initial assessment and prepare for an in-person inspection.
  • Coordinate collision repairs, insurance-related communications and requested services.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website and virtual assistant.
  • Measure website performance and understand which pages and contact options are useful.
  • Maintain business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Photos and online information support an initial review only. An in-person inspection is required for an accurate estimate.

4. Service providers and disclosures

We may share information with vendors that help us operate the website and handle inquiries, including website hosting, analytics, image storage and artificial-intelligence providers. These currently include Vercel, Google Analytics, Cloudinary and Groq. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

We may also disclose information to insurers, adjusters, towing providers, parts suppliers or other repair-related parties when you request or authorize coordination; to comply with law or protect safety and rights; or as part of a business transfer. We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Virtual assistant and automated tools

Messages sent to the virtual assistant may be processed by an artificial-intelligence provider to generate a response and help route your inquiry. The assistant provides general information and does not make final decisions about estimates, insurance coverage, repair authorization, safety or eligibility for deductible assistance. Do not submit Social Security numbers, payment-card details, passwords, medical information or other unnecessary sensitive data through the assistant or forms.

6. Analytics, cookies and similar technologies

We use essential browser storage for site functions and Google Analytics to measure visits and interactions. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. Browser settings can limit cookies, although some features may work differently. We have disabled Google signals and advertising-personalization signals in our website analytics configuration.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, business records, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Retention may vary by the type of inquiry or repair. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. If a security incident requires notification, we will provide notice as required by applicable law.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask to access, correct or delete information you submitted, or object to certain uses, by contacting us. We will verify and review the request and respond as required by applicable law. Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for an active repair, legal obligation, security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution. You may also contact us at any time to stop nonessential follow-up communications.

9. Children’s privacy

The website is intended for vehicle owners and people arranging vehicle repairs. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted information may contact us to request its deletion.

10. External links and policy changes

The website may link to maps, social networks or other third-party services. Their privacy practices are outside our control. We may update this policy if our services, technology or legal obligations change. The revised policy becomes effective when posted, unless a later date is stated, and the date above identifies the latest version.
