Straight answers

Questions people ask before trusting us with their car.

How long will my repair take?+

Timing depends on damage, parts and insurance approval. After inspection we provide a realistic estimate and keep you updated.

Will it look like it did before?+

Our goal is to restore the vehicle's structure, shape and finish through professional body and refinishing processes.

Can you help with my deductible?+

Depending on the repair and claim, deductible waiver or assistance may be available. We review each case individually.

Do you work with insurance companies?+

Yes. We can document the damage and help you understand the repair process. You retain the right to choose your repair facility.

Can I choose my own body shop?+

Generally, vehicle owners can choose their repair facility. Contact your insurer about the specific terms of your claim.

What should I send for an assessment?+

Send your contact information, vehicle year/make/model, claim status, a short description and clear photos of the visible damage.

Do I need an appointment?+

Calling or submitting your information first helps the team prepare and confirm the best time for an inspection.

Do you repair serious structural damage?+

Yes. The shop can evaluate significant collision damage and has frame-machine capability for structural correction when appropriate.
